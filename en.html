01 / POWER ELECTRONICS2026/03 — 2026/06

GD32G553-based two-stage DC fast charging system

Control algorithm and embedded software development

  • Developed control algorithms for a T-type three-level rectifier and a three-phase interleaved Buck converter, using voltage-current dual-loop control and neutral-point voltage balancing for the rectifier and voltage-loop control for the Buck stage.
  • Wrote the DSP and supporting FPGA programs for coordinated control of the front-end rectifier.
  • Developed GD32G553 software for the downstream Buck stage, implementing ADC voltage sampling, PI control, and three PWM outputs interleaved by 120°.

02 / POWER ELECTRONICS2025/11 — 2026/04

Design and study of an active power filter

Undergraduate thesis · Independently completed hardware and software design and thesis writing

  • Designed a three-phase two-level shunt active power filter for nonlinear-load harmonic compensation, covering the power circuit, gate-driver interfaces, signal sampling and conditioning circuits, and PCB layouts.
  • Implemented dq harmonic detection and SRF-PLL phase locking, and compared PI, multiple proportional-resonant, and proportional-repetitive control (PRC) in MATLAB/Simulink. With PRC + SVPWM, steady-state simulated grid-current THD fell from 19.21% to 3.16%.
  • Developed C software in CCS for the TMS320F28335, implementing sampling, phase locking, the DC-voltage outer loop, the PRC current loop, and SVPWM for real-time closed-loop control at 10 kHz.
  • Built a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) validation platform using a DSP and Typhoon HIL. With a three-phase diode rectifier load, grid-current THD fell from 20.15% to 4.86%, while the HIL model's DC bus remained regulated at 800 V.
